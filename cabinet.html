CalCar memory The assistant remembers what matters from your chats: name, budget, what you are looking for. You can edit or erase it
Analyze our past conversations in which we discussed choosing, buying or comparing cars. I may not have stated my requirements directly. I may have simply sent listing links, asked "what do you think of this car?", compared different cars, said why I like or dislike something, rejected options, discussed price, mileage, accidents, equipment, engines, electric cars, maintenance and other related things. Based on this correspondence, build my profile as a car buyer. Use only what actually follows from our conversations. Do not invent anything. Determine, if it was actually discussed: - budget and acceptable price range; - the relevant country/market; - how the car will be used; - brands, models and types of cars I am interested in; - cars I have already considered; - what I liked about them; - why I rejected specific options; - the characteristics that matter most to me; - desirable parameters; - deal-breakers; - attitude to mileage and age; - attitude to accidents and rebuilt cars; - preferences on power and dynamics; - EV / hybrid / ICE preferences; - attitude to equipment, comfort, reliability and maintenance cost; - any recurring preferences that are really visible from our communication. Split the result into: 1. Confirmed preferences - what I said explicitly. 2. Likely preferences - conclusions from my recurring behaviour and choices. Always mark them as assumptions. 3. Deal-breakers / what does not suit me. 4. Cars I have already considered, and what I liked or disliked about them. 5. Current context of choice, if it is clear from our conversations what I am looking for now. If there is no information, do not add it. If the information is contradictory, say so. Do not fill gaps with guesses. Return a compact but sufficiently detailed structured profile in English. It will be pasted in full into the memory of my car AI assistant.
